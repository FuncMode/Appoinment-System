# Anti-Slop Audit 001 — MedicaCare Public Pages

- **Petsa:** 2026-09-15
- **Reference:** [miqdadbadjuber/anti-slop](https://github.com/miqdadbadjuber/anti-slop) (`antislop.md`, Mode 2 audit)
- **Saklaw:** Lahat ng public pages — Landing, Services, Doctors, About, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Login, Register, Forgot Password, Admin Login (`#/admin/login`) — kasama ang shared na `PublicNav`, `PublicFooter`, `NoticeBar`, `ClinicStatus`, `TestimonialCarousel`, `FaqAccordion`, at ang `styles.css` na ginagamit nila.
- **Paano nag-audit:** Static code inspection ng JSX + CSS, token contrast computation, at `npm run build` + 31-route smoke test (pumasa lahat). Hindi pa naisasagawa ang buong manual click-through (R-35, tingnan ang #14).
- **Tandaan:** Ayon sa anti-slop Mode 2, **walang gagalawin** hanggang piliin mo ang mga numerong aayusin. Priority ay sumusunod sa rule tier: Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW.

---

## HIGH — Hard Gate (absolute rules)

**1. R-02 — Em dash (`—`) sa UI text (±15 instances sa public pages).**
Hindi pwede ang em dash sa anumang text; comma/period/colon/parentheses ang gamit. Nakita sa:
Landing h1 (`"Book a MedicaCare specialist online — no phone calls needed."`), Landing CTA h2 (`"Skip the phone queue — book online"`), How-it-works card 3 (`"…easy rescheduling — all in your portal."`), care-finder panel (`"…right now — bookings open as early as this week."`), 2 Landing FAQ answers (`"Yes — create a free patient account…"`, `"Yes — executive check-up bundles…"`), Services card 2 (`"…instant confirmation — no phone calls needed."`), Services FAQ 4 (`"Yes — executive check-up bundles…"`), About "Who we are" (dalawang em dash sa isang paragraph), About values (`"We treat people, not just charts — clear explanations…"`), Forgot Password quote (`"…prescriptions — all in one secure place."`), NoticeBar (`"our ER never closes — walk in anytime"`), map iframe title (`"Map — MedicaCare location"`), footer disclaimer (`"(IPT2) only — MedicaCare is a fictional hospital…"`, `components.jsx` ~line 497), nav brand title/aria labels (`"MedicaCare — back to home page"`), at testimonial attribution prefix (`"— {t.who}"`). *(Ang em dash sa code comments ay exempt — hindi UI text.)*

**2. R-03 — Mga tap target na mas maliit sa 44px (mobile requirement).**
- `.testimonial-dot`: **8×8px** (pinakamaliit; walang padding hit-area)
- `.demo-accounts-toggle`: `padding: 0`; ~17px lang ang taas ng 11px title text
- `.chip` (care-guide symptom chips): `padding: 6px 12px` ≈ **31px** taas
- `.btn`: **36px**; `.btn.lg`: **42px** (1–2px na lang kulang)
- `.testimonial-arrow`: 34×34px; `.public-nav-burger`: 38×38px
OK na ang `.dept-chip` (~48px) at `.faq-question` (~48px). Ang layout flow (walang horizontal overflow, mobile doctors list + pagination, 6 breakpoints) ay maayos sa code inspection, pero hindi pa visually na-verify sa device.

**3. R-26 + R-32 — Ang `.demo-account` rows sa Login at Admin Login ay hindi keyboard-operable.**
Clickable `<div onClick>` lang — walang `role="button"`, `tabIndex`, o Enter/Space handler, kaya hindi maabot ng Tab at hindi ma-activate gamit ang keyboard. Contrast: ang `.doctor-card` sa Doctors page ay tama (`role="button" tabIndex={0}` + `onKeyDown`). Ito ang tanging interactive element sa auth pages na dead sa keyboard.

**4. R-17 + R-38 — Mga gawa-gawang statistics na naka-present bilang totoo.**
Hero stats: `"< 12 min"` avg wait at `"4.8 / 5"` patient rating (walang pinagmulan); About stats: `"120+ specialists"` (12 lang sa app), `"18 departments"` (**direktang kontradiksyon** sa Landing na live ang `{SPECIALTIES.length}` = 10), `"35 yrs"`. *May existing mitigation:* malinaw na fictional disclaimer sa footer ng bawat public page. Pero sa loob mismo ng page, naka-present pa rin sila bilang marketing claims nang walang `[REAL DATA]` o katumbas na label. Pinakamabigat ang 120+ vs 12 at 18 vs 10 na pagkakasalungat.

**5. R-36 — Gawa-gawang security/compliance claims.**
Hero badges: `"DOH accredited"`, `"24/7 patient support"`. Pinakamabigat: **Privacy Policy page** nagsasabi na `"encryption in transit and at rest"`, `"Passwords are stored only as secure hashes"`, `"every access is logged and audited"` — lahat ay **hindi totoo** sa kasalukuyang prototype (plain-text passwords sa `localStorage`, walang backend). Kahit may fictional disclaimer, masyadong spesipiko ang mga claim. Alinman sa (a) i-align sa katotohanan ng prototype, o (b) i-label bilang target ng production system.

## MEDIUM — Purpose-Gate (allowed, pero kailangan ng nakasulat na dahilan)

**7. R-14 — Mga card na magkakapareho lahat, walang nakasulat na hierarchy reason.**
Services: 6 identical cards (same icon-chip + padding + layout); About "What we stand for": 3 identical cards; Landing How-it-works: 3 identical cards. Walang nakasulat na dahilan kung bakit uniform. Ang code comment sa hero stats (`"no icon chips, the value itself is the information"`) ay nagpapakita na kaya namang gawin ito nang may intent.

**8. R-08 — Decorative arrows (`→`) sa maraming button, walang nakasulat na purpose.**
Hero CTA `"Create patient account →"`, care panel `"See Cardiology doctors →"`, lahat ng 10 dept-chips, doctor-card `"View profile →"`, modal `"Book with this doctor →"`, `"Open larger map →"`. Ang arrow ng dept-chip ay may navigation meaning; ang iba ay pure decoration na.

**9. R-01 — Radial glow circles sa auth visual columns, walang nakasulat na dahilan.**
`styles.css` ~863-864: dalawang `radial-gradient` (8% / 6% white) sa auth photo columns — decoration lang. (Ang linear blue gradient overlay mismo ay **PASA** — may nakasulat na dahilan sa comment: text readability sa ibabaw ng photos.)

**10. R-23 — randomuser.me portraits bilang mukha ng mga doktor sa public Doctors page.**
Mga stock photo ng tao na naka-present bilang mga doktor ng ospital. Nakadisclose sa footer disclaimer at sa code comments ("dummy portrait"), at may working na initials-avatar fallback ang `DoctorAvatar` kung mabigo ang pag-load. Sa public marketing page, i-consider ang initials-avatar (suportado na) o photo credit/label.

**11. R-37 — Hindi kailanman nadeklara ang mga dial; walang `DESIGN.md`.**
May direction (DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md, design handoff, at design tokens na may documented contrast reasons — magandang pundasyon ito), pero hindi kailanman naisulat ang ENERGY/RHYTHM/MOTION dials o ang Design Read. Mungkahi: gumawa ng maikling `DESIGN.md` na may `Dial: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1` (healthcare portal = kalmado; RHYTHM 2 kung itutuloy ang kasalukuyang halo ng care-finder/stats/FAQ sections). Isulat din dito ang mga dahilan para sa IBM Plex, Lucide, at fixed light theme (tingnan ang #15).

## LOW — Quality Locks (consistency)

**12. R-05 — AI-template na "How it works" (eksaktong 3 steps) + uniform na section rhythm.**
Landing: "How it works" = 3 numbered identical cards (explicit na banned default sa R-05), at ~5 sunud-sunod na sections na may parehong h2 + sub + grid na pattern. Alinman sa i-varied ang composition, o ideklara ang RHYTHM 1 bilang pinili (hindi aksidente).

**13. R-11 — Radius consistency: PASA pero naka-note.**
Buttons/cards = 6-10px, chips = pill (`--r-pill`), avatars = circle. May variation at may design-system tokens — PASA. Walang pill-everything pattern.

**14. R-35 — Verification evidence (honest status):**
- PASA: `npm run build` (39 modules, walang error) at `node scripts/smoke.mjs` (31/31 routes rendered, kasama ang `/admin/login` at `/forgot-password`) — naisagawa ngayong araw.
- PASA (code inspection): lahat ng public controls ay may totoong gawain maliban sa `.demo-account` (#3) — nav links (R-24 PASA), care finder chips, FAQ accordions, testimonial controls, forms na may validation + feedback, modal na nagsasara.
- **HINDI PA GINAWA:** buong manual click-through sa browser kada breakpoint, at console error check. Gawin ito bago ang depensa.

**15. Mga PASA na may dahilan (walang aksyon, naka-document lang):**
- R-15 CTAs: lahat spesipiko ("Create patient account", "See Cardiology doctors", "Book with this doctor") — PASA
- R-16 buzzwords: zero hits sa scan (seamless/effortless/cutting-edge/AI-powered atbp.) — PASA
- R-25 contrast: `--text-muted` #6B7280 = 4.83:1 sa white, ≈4.6:1 sa `--bg` #F5F7FA (AA pasa; may documented non-text contrast reasoning pa sa tokens) — PASA; i-verify lang visually ang white quote text sa pinakamaliwanag na bahagi ng auth photos
- R-21/R-34: fixed light theme, walang toggle — may healthcare reason pero isulat sa DESIGN.md (#11)
- R-28 FAQ: product-specific lahat (HMO, lab cut-off, 24h cancellation) — PASA
- R-30: Cleveland Clinic/Mayo/ZocDoc inspiration ay naka-document sa comments, hindi clone — PASA
- R-07/R-10/R-12/R-13: walang background grids, walang glassmorphism, shadows ay selective (nav-scrolled/modal/drawer lang), glow ay 1 element lang — PASA
- R-33: walang patch scripts sa repo — PASA
- R-27: Doctors page ay may loading skeleton + empty state + clear-filters; forms ay may inline errors — PASA
